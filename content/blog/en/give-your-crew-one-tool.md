**If you run a crew of ABN contractors, half your week isn't the work — it's chasing the paperwork around it.**

The invoice that came in wrong, or didn't come in at all. The subbie who's stressed about tax and starts eyeing a full-time gig. The new person who takes three weeks to get their admin sorted before they're really up and running.

None of it is your job, exactly. But it all lands on your desk anyway.

## A different way to handle it

Instead of leaving every contractor to figure out their own admin, you give them one tool that just handles it — invoices, tax set-aside, the lot — and you cover it for them.

What the business gets back is the quiet stuff that actually matters:

- Invoices that come in right the first time
- Contractors who feel looked after, so they stick around
- New people productive on day one, not week three
