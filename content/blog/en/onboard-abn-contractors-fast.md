Onboarding ABN contractors without paperwork chaos is key to reducing the admin load for businesses, with the average cost of onboarding a new contractor ranging from $500 to $2,000.
If you run a cleaning company with 15 contractors, the cost of onboarding can quickly add up, and the paperwork required for each contractor can be overwhelming, including verifying their [ABN registration](https://www.ato.gov.au/) and ensuring they have the necessary [visa documentation](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/).
The #1 thing operators tell us is that they struggle to keep track of all the paperwork and ensure compliance with [tax laws](https://www.ato.gov.au/) and [immigration regulations](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/).
To onboard ABN contractors efficiently, businesses can use a streamlined onboarding process that includes:
Checking contractor credentials
Verifying ABN registration
Ensuring necessary visa documentation
Providing clear payment terms and [invoice requirements](https://www.ato.gov.au/)
