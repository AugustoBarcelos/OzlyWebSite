**Calling someone a "contractor" doesn't make them one — and getting it wrong can land your business with back-pay, super, and penalties.**

If you run on ABN contractors — cleaning, trades, delivery — this is the compliance risk that quietly grows under you. A worker you've always treated as a contractor can be ruled an *employee* by the [ATO](https://www.ato.gov.au/) or [Fair Work](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/), and the bill is retrospective.

## It's not about the label — or even the ABN

Two things people get wrong:

1. **An ABN doesn't settle it.** A worker having an ABN and sending invoices doesn't automatically make them a contractor. The whole working relationship is what counts.
2. **The written contract matters more than it used to.** Since the 2022 High Court decisions, the terms of a genuine written agreement carry real weight — but only if they reflect what actually happens day to day.

## What's actually weighed

No single factor decides it. The ATO and Fair Work look at the relationship as a whole:

| Points to **employee** | Points to **contractor** |
|---|---|
| You control how/when the work is done | They decide how the work gets done |
| They must do the work personally | They can delegate or subcontract |
| Paid for time (hourly, ongoing) | Paid for a result / per job |
| You provide tools & equipment | They provide their own |
| They bear no commercial risk | They carry their own risk & insurance |
| Integrated into your business | Running their own business |

> **The #1 thing operators tell us:** the worker "felt like one of the team." That feeling is exactly what tips the scale toward *employee*.

## What it costs if you're wrong

Reclassification isn't a slap on the wrist. It can mean:

- **Back-payment** of wages, leave and entitlements
- **Superannuation guarantee** owed (plus charges and interest)
- **PAYG** withholding you should have deducted
- **Sham-contracting penalties** under the Fair Work Act — civil penalties that run into the tens of thousands per contravention

One audit can convert a year of "savings" into a much bigger liability.

## How to stay clean

Genuine contractors look and operate like real businesses. Help yours do exactly that:

- They have their own ABN and **invoice you** (not a timesheet you fill in)
- They control **how** the work is done and can knock back jobs
- They can **subcontract** or send someone else
- They carry their **own insurance** and supply their own gear
- Everyone keeps **clean records** of the arrangement

That last point is where most disputes are won or lost — documentation that shows a genuine business-to-business relationship.
